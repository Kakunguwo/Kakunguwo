## About Me

I am a software developer and biomedical engineer focused on building practical, scalable systems across healthcare, data infrastructure, artificial intelligence and automation.

My work combines full-stack engineering with biomedical domain knowledge, particularly in medical imaging systems, PACS, RIS, DICOM workflows, healthcare interoperability and AI-assisted clinical tools.

Beyond healthcare, I also work on enterprise platforms, data pipelines, workflow automation, IoT systems and intelligent software products designed for African and resource-constrained environments.

### What I Bring

* **Full-Stack Engineering** — Building production-ready applications from frontend interfaces to backend services and infrastructure
* **Healthcare Software** — Developing PACS, RIS, DICOM, medical imaging and clinical workflow systems
* **Backend Architecture** — Designing APIs, modular services, databases, authentication and real-time systems
* **AI Integration** — Applying machine learning, computer vision, speech processing and language models to practical products
* **Data Engineering** — Building data collection, transformation, storage and orchestration pipelines
* **Biomedical Engineering** — Connecting software, medical devices, sensors and healthcare workflows
* **Product Thinking** — Translating real operational problems into maintainable technical solutions
